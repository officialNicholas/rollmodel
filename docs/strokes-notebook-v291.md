# Strokes: categories and details, two different things (v291)

![The Strokes page before (dark), in v287 (paper, but the categories and the strokes looked alike), and now: coloured construction-paper category cards over a taped lined notebook page with the strokes as stickers, the equipped one ringed in marker, a craft on the bench as a sticky note, and landscape](strokes-notebook-v291.jpg)

Built in `tools/v291_strokes_notebook.py` (on top of v287), with a new paper grain `game/art/m_cpaper_g.webp` (made from
`m_cpaper.webp`).

- **Categories (Special, Skill, Transform):** cards of coloured construction paper, gold, blue and red, each named by a strip of
  masking tape. The open one sits up straight and lifted with a paper arrow down to its page; the other two sit back, a shade darker.
- **What's in it:** one sheet of lined notebook paper (pale blue rules, a red margin line), taped down at its top corners. The heading
  is written on it in ink with a swipe of highlighter in the category's colour, its note under it.
- **The strokes:** stickers on the page with their names written under them. The equipped one is ringed in marker. Ones still to
  craft or locked are faded stickers with their time or rank; More soon is a pencilled box.
- **A craft on the bench:** a yellow sticky note, taped at its top, with Skip on tape.
- The card that opens from a sticker is unchanged (paper sheet, stroke buttons).
