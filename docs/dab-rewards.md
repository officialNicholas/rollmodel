# How many dabs a match pays (game v414)

A match pays more the harder it was. Every match is worked out the same way:

```
dabs = (mode base + paint + place + KOs + most KOs) x challenge  +  rings + new best
```

Then Jackpot (the skill) adds half the match's dabs again on a win.

## The parts

| Part | Time trial | 1v1 | 3-way | Showdown 2v2 | Showdown (free for all) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mode base | 6 | 8 | 10 | 10 | 12 |
| Paint | a third of your % | a third of your % | a third of your % | none | none |
| Place | none | win 30 | 1st 34, 2nd 12 | win 30 | 1st 40, 2nd 14, 3rd 5 |
| Draw for first | none | 12 | 14 | 12 | 16 |
| Each KO | none | +4 | +4 | +5 | +5 |
| Most KOs (yours alone) | none | +12 | +12 | +12 | +12 |
| Rings | +2 each | none | none | none | none |
| New best | +10 | +10 | +10 | none | none |

The paint part counts your coverage, so 35% painted adds 12.

## Challenge

| Who you play | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| CPUs on easy | x0.75 |
| CPUs on medium | x1 |
| CPUs on hard | x1.35 |
| Real people online | x1.5 |
| The lessons | x1 |
| Time trial | none (rings and paint pay instead) |

The multiplier covers the base, paint, place and KOs. It does not cover rings or a new best.

## Typical matches

These assume 40% paint in the painting modes and no new best. The painting modes assume no KOs. The Showdown wins assume 2 KOs and the most KOs, and the Showdown losses assume 4th place with no KOs.

| Match | Win | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Time trial, no rings | 20 | 20 |
| Time trial, 8 rings | 36 | 36 |
| Time trial, 15 rings | 50 | 50 |
| 1v1, easy CPU | 39 | 17 |
| 1v1, medium CPU (same as before) | 52 | 22 |
| 1v1, hard CPU | 70 | 30 |
| 1v1 online | 78 | 33 |
| 3-way, medium (2nd: 36) | 58 | 24 (3rd) |
| 3-way, hard (2nd: 49) | 78 | 32 (3rd) |
| Showdown, medium | 74 | 12 |
| Showdown, hard | 100 | 16 |
| Showdown online | 111 | 18 |

In the painting modes, each KO adds 4 before the multiplier, and the most KOs (yours alone) adds another 12.

The top payouts are a Showdown free for all won against people online with the most KOs, then the same against hard CPUs.

## Rank ups

Each rank you reach pays **25 + 5 x the new rank**:

| Rank reached | Dabs |
|---|---|
| 2 | 35 |
| 5 | 50 |
| 10 | 75 |
| 20 | 125 |
| 30 | 175 |

The dabs fly up to your dabs counter on the results. A rank up from a commission's XP pays the same, on the commission's own coin flight.

## Before v414

- **1v1, 3-way and Time trial:** 8 + a third of your paint + 30 for a win (12 for a draw) + 10 for a new best. A 3-way paid 6 more.
- **Showdown:** 8 + 5 per KO + 30 for a win (12 for a draw) + 8 for second place.
- Easy, medium and hard paid the same.
- Online paid the same as a CPU match.
- Rings paid nothing, and a rank up paid nothing.

## Per hour

This assumes about 20 matches an hour, half won and half lost.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Medium CPUs | about 740 | about 740 (unchanged) |
| Easy CPUs | about 740 | about 560 |
| Hard CPUs | about 740 | about 1,000 |
| Online | about 740 | about 1,100 |
| Rank ups, mid ranks | 0 | about +10 a match |

A skin at 1,250 to 1,500 dabs takes about one and a half to two hours on medium. On hard or online it's a little over an hour.
