## Description (4000 max, 2682 characters)

```
Fast, splashy paint battles made for mobile! Roll, swim and splat your color across the canvas in 90-second matches. Paint the most ground before the clock runs out and the canvas is yours.

Pick your color, gear up your axolotl and take on rivals in Versus, Showdown, Time trial and live online 1v1s. Grab wild Transforms, craft Strokes like the Rocket and Flash Freeze, and dress to impress with hats, eyes and more from the Paint Shop.


◉ BATTLE IN MULTIPLE MODES

▶ Versus (1v1 or 3-way): Go head to head with one rival or two. Whoever paints the most of the canvas in 90 seconds wins.

▶ Play online (1v1): Take your paint skills online and battle real players live.

▶ Showdown (free-for-all or 2v2): Two hearts each and a zone that shrinks every minute. Knock rivals out and be the last one standing, solo or with a teammate.

▶ Time trial: Just you and 60 seconds. Paint as much as you can and post your best on each canvas's weekly leaderboard.


◉ ROLL, SPLAT AND POUND

Swipe to steer, then jump, dodge roll and dash. Pull back to fling yourself like a slingshot, then ground pound anyone who gets too close. Low on paint? Dive into a paint basin to refill, hide, and burst out when they least expect it.


◉ TRANSFORMS

Turn the match around in a splat! Flatten rivals with the Roller, blast them with the Turret, set a sticky Paint Trap, spin up a Whirlwind or let a Boomerang fly.


◉ STROKES: SPECIALS AND SKILLS

Craft and equip game-changing Strokes. Blast off with the Rocket, stomp around as a Giant or freeze every rival in place with Flash Freeze. Add a skill like Swim faster, Weather Repel or Deep Pockets to match your play style.


◉ 7 CANVASES TO SPLAT

Blank Canvas, Palette Island, Sludge Sewer, Fishing Raft, The Crypt, The Cathedral and The Manor. Dodge the scorching sun, splash through the rain and grab the orb to go giant!


◉ DRESS TO IMPRESS

Make your axolotl your own in the Locker. Pick from 6 paint colors, eye styles from Sly to Zombie, and 25 wearables like the Pirate hat, Top hat, Hero cape and Propeller cap. Earn dabs every match and spend them in the Paint Shop.


◉ COMMISSIONS AND RANKS

Take on daily Commissions for bonus dabs, climb the ranks and unlock new modes and Strokes as you go.


◉ SEASON 1: SPOOKY SZN

Halloween has hit the canvas! Splat your way through The Crypt, The Cathedral and The Manor, and grab spooky gear like the Hockey mask, Witch hat, Skull bow and Zombie eyes.


◉ PLAY WITH FRIENDS

Add friends with a friend code, see who's online and invite them to a private match.


◉ PLAY ANYWHERE

Versus, Showdown and Time trial work offline, so you can splat on the go.


See you on the canvas!

A Marcelin Game
```
