How it plays

Own the canvas

Every match is a race to cover the canvas. Roll over the floor to paint it your color, and over your rivals’ color to take it back. When the clock runs out, the most paint wins.

  • Move

    Steer, jump, roll

    Drag sideways to steer. Tap to jump, swipe up to roll. Your own color is a fast lane that refills your paint.

  • Pound

    Ground pound

    Pound to splat a circle of paint. Any rival inside is out for a few seconds, so jump it or time a roll.

  • Fling

    Slingshot

    Hold to stop, pull down and let go to fling across the canvas. Pound mid-fling to fire a missile that steers itself.

  • The orb

    Go giant

    Grab the glowing orb for a super move. Grow huge for 5 seconds and roll right over anyone in your way.

  • Transforms

    Transform

    Pick up a roller, a turret, a paint trap, a whirlwind or a boomerang, and change into it when the moment’s right.

  • Weather

    Heat and rain

    A heat wave bakes anyone out in the open, so hide in a paint tub. Rain speeds everyone up and waters paint down.

Modes

Pick your fight

Take on the CPU, a crowd, the clock, or your friends.

  • 1v1 or 3-way

    Versus CPU

    You against one rival, or two. Ninety seconds on the clock, and whoever covers the most canvas wins.

    • 1 v 1
    • 3-way
  • Last one standing

    Showdown

    Four of you on a bigger canvas, three hearts each. A knock-out costs a heart, and with none left you’re out.

    • Free-for-all
    • 2 v 2
  • Weekly leaderboard

    Time trial

    Just you and 60 seconds. Cover all you can, beat your best, and climb each canvas’s board before the week is up.

    • Solo
    • A new board every week
  • Online

    Online 1v1

    Add your friends with their friend code, send an invite, and settle it on the canvas.

    • Friends
    • Invites

The Paint Shop and Strokes

Make it yours

Earn dabs, dress up your axolotl, and craft the moves you bring to every match.

Dress for the splat

Win dabs in every match and spend them in the Paint Shop on hats, glasses, bow ties, capes and more. Then pick your paint color and your eyes in My Locker.

  • No real money. Everything’s bought with dabs you earn.
  • Paint colors, eyes, hats, glasses and capes.
  • Spooky SZN finds to hunt down on the season’s canvases.

Craft your strokes

Strokes are your specials and skills. Rank up to open new ones, then craft them on the bench. Pick a special for when you grab the orb, a skill that’s on all match, and the transform you start every match holding.
