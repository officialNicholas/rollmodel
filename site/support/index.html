Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Make an account in Settings › Account. While you’re signed in, your progress is saved with your account. On the new phone, open Settings › Account and log in with your username and password.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play on your own without one, and your progress is saved on your phone. You need an account (13 and older) to play online and add friends.

I forgot my password.

Email us from any address and tell us your username. We’ll help you get back in.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings › Account › Delete account. It removes your account and everything kept with it from our server for good. You can also email us and we’ll do it for you.

How do I report a player or a username?

Block them from your Friends screen, then email us their username and what happened. We can change or remove usernames that break the Terms.

Ads and privacy

Ads only play when you tap to watch one. Where the law asks for it, the Privacy Policy in the app’s Settings has an “Ad privacy choices” button. Our Privacy Policy explains what’s kept.
