Privacy Policy

Updated October 16, 2026.

On this device

Your progress (your dabs, items, ranks and settings) is saved on this device. You don’t need an account to play.

Your username

Your username is kept on our server so no one else can take it. Other players can see it, with your paint colour and rank.

Your account

If you make an account, we keep your username, your first and last name, your birth year, and when you agreed to these terms. Your name and birth year are private: other players never see them. We ask for your birth year because online play is for players 13 and older.

While you’re signed in, we also keep a copy of your game progress (your dabs, items, ranks, unlocks and lessons done) with your account, so you can log in on another phone and pick up where you left off. Log out leaves it with your account and starts this device fresh.

Your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase. It’s stored scrambled (hashed), never in a form anyone can read, not even us.

Playing online

To run friends and online matches we keep your friend code, your friends, friend requests and blocks, invites to play, your Time trial scores for the friends board, records of your online matches, and when you were last online.

Who has it

We don’t sell your information. Supabase stores it for us, and online matches run on our game server.

Ads

In the iPhone app you can choose to watch an ad, for example to finish crafting a stroke right away. An ad only plays when you tap to watch one. Ads come from Google AdMob, which gets some information from your device to show an ad and count it: your IP address (which gives a rough location), the kind of device, an identifier for the device that’s only shared within our apps, and how the ad was watched. The game doesn’t ask to track you across other companies’ apps. If you told us you’re under 13, you only get ads that are fit for children, and they aren’t personalised. Where the law asks for it, Google asks for your consent before your first ad.

Deleting it

Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account and everything above from our server for good. Restart Game in Settings clears this device.

Contact
